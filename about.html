About Lootera.win

A lottery-first platform for choosing numbers, taking tickets, following draws and viewing winner history.

What this project is

Lootera.win is currently a lottery draw and ticket simulation/testing platform. Authenticated users can join open lotteries, choose their own numbers, confirm lottery tickets with virtual Draw Credits, keep ticket history and follow ranked draw results.

How lotteries work

  • Every lottery maintains its own isolated pool of submitted tickets.
  • Players choose numbers manually or use Quick Pick when available.
  • When a lottery is drawn, the backend selects winners only from tickets already submitted to that lottery.
  • Ticket purchases, prizes and admin balance adjustments are recorded in the Draw Credit ledger.
  • Love Points (LP) remain separate from lottery participation and cannot be used to buy tickets or improve winning odds.

Technology

The public frontend is hosted on GitHub Pages. Authentication, database services and server-authoritative draw functions are provided through Supabase, with Google OAuth used for account sign-in.

Important limitation

The current system uses virtual Draw Credits for simulation/testing. Draw Credits have no cash value and cannot be withdrawn. No cash prize or cash-redeemable gameplay credit is currently offered through the lottery system.
