Lootera.win demonstrates lottery ticket creation, scheduled or admin-triggered draws, ranked winner selection and result history. References to tickets, prizes, jackpots or credits describe virtual testing values unless the service is explicitly updated under applicable legal and regulatory requirements.

Accounts

You may use Google sign-in to create an authenticated session. You are responsible for access to your Google account and for activity performed through your authenticated session.

Virtual Draw Credits

Draw Credits are internal simulation units with no cash value. They may be used by enabled lottery-ticket features. They cannot be withdrawn, redeemed for money, transferred for cash value or treated as a financial return.

Lottery tickets and draw results

Submitted lottery tickets are digital test records. Each lottery maintains its own ticket pool. When a lottery is drawn, the backend selects the configured number of winner tickets only from tickets already submitted to that lottery and applies the configured rank-specific virtual-credit prizes.

Development support and Love Points

The project may accept voluntary development support outside the lottery system. A linked Phone Bridge may verify a received-money bKash notification by matching the sender number and TrxID. A verified transfer can create Love Points. Love Points are a supporter record only: they cannot purchase lottery tickets, increase winning odds, create prize eligibility, be withdrawn, be transferred for value or be converted to Draw Credits.

No real-money wagering in the current build

The current build does not sell cash-redeemable Draw Credits, accept wagers for monetary returns or provide cash prizes. If the project later includes regulated real-money lottery activity, separate legal, licensing, age, identity, payment and jurisdictional requirements would apply before such functionality could be offered.
