Privacy Policy

How Lootera.win handles account, lottery-ticket, referral and development-support verification data.

Last updated: October 6, 2026

Lootera.win currently uses virtual Draw Credits for lottery simulation/testing. Draw Credits have no cash value and cannot be withdrawn. Separate development-support transfers may be verified only to record Love Points (LP).

Information we collect

If you sign in with Google, the authentication provider may provide your name, email address, profile image and a unique account identifier. We also store lottery tickets, selected numbers, lottery results, profile/referral records and virtual-credit ledger movements associated with your account.

Development-support verification

If the Phone Bridge feature is used, the system processes a received-money bKash SMS/notification to identify the transfer amount, TrxID, sender number and received time. The full SMS text is not stored in the transaction database. The sender number is normalized and stored as a cryptographic hash plus only its last four digits.

How we use information

  • To authenticate your account and associate lottery tickets with you.
  • To display ticket history, balances, referrals and lottery results.
  • To verify development-support transfers and prevent duplicate claims.
  • To operate, secure, troubleshoot and improve the application.
  • To prevent unauthorized access to another user's private records.

Public and private data

General lottery information and public draw results may be visible publicly. Account-linked tickets, balances, referral records, Love Point balances, support claims, device tokens and transaction-verification records are protected through authenticated access controls or server-side administration.

Service providers

The application uses Google for OAuth sign-in, Supabase for authentication/database/server functions and GitHub Pages for website hosting. bKash is not integrated as a lottery payment gateway. The Phone Bridge is used only for the separate development-support record system.

Data retention

Account, ticket, ledger, referral, support-verification and Love Point records may remain stored while this testing project is active. Records may also be reset or deleted during development and testing.

Security

We use authentication, row-level database access controls, server-side functions, transaction locking, hashed device tokens, hashed sender numbers and server-authoritative lottery draw logic to reduce unauthorized access and manipulation. No internet service can guarantee absolute security.

Children

This application is intended for general software testing and demonstration and is not directed to children.

Your choices

You may stop using the service at any time. To request removal of account-linked test data, contact the project owner through the public GitHub repository.

Changes

This policy may be updated as the application changes. The latest version will remain available at this URL.

Contact

For privacy questions, use the public contact information linked from the About & Contact page.
