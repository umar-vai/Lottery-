REFERRAL Q&A

How sharing works

How do I invite someone?

Copy or share the personal link shown above. The referral code is included automatically in the link.

When do I receive the 3 bonus Draw Credits?

The person must be a genuinely new DRAW//01 account and the referral must attach during the new-account window. Once linked successfully, 3 virtual Draw Credits are added to your main Draw Credit balance and recorded in the credit ledger.

Can an existing player use my referral code?

No referral bonus is created for an established account. A user can have only one referrer, and self-referrals are blocked.

How does the Support Points referral reward work?

Support rewards are cumulative. For every full 10 Support Points settled by a user who joined through your link, you receive 1 Support Point. For example, 5 SP and later another 5 SP reaches 10 total and earns 1 SP.

Are Support Points the same as Draw Credits?

No. They remain completely separate. Support Points cannot buy tickets, change odds, create prize eligibility, be converted into Draw Credits, or be withdrawn as cash.

Can I earn more than one signup bonus from the same person?

No. Each referred account can create the signup reward only once.

Where can I see my referral results?

The statistics and player list above update from the server. You can see joined users, signup credits earned, referred Support Points and your Support Points referral reward.
