Privacy Policy

How Game Zone handles account, event-ticket, instant-game, referral, and development-support verification data.

Last updated: October 5, 2026

Game Zone is a simulation/testing project. Draw Credits have no cash value and cannot be withdrawn. Separate development-support transfers may be verified only to record non-gameplay Support Points.

Information we collect

If you sign in with Google, the authentication provider may provide basic account information such as your name, email address, profile image, and a unique account identifier. We also store simulated event tickets, profile/referral records, virtual-credit ledger movements, and instant-game spin history such as bet amount, reel result, multiplier, payout, and balance before/after.

Development-support verification

If the Phone Bridge feature is used, the system processes a received-money bKash SMS/notification to identify the transfer amount, TrxID, sender number, and received time. The full SMS text is not stored in the transaction database. The sender number is normalized and stored as a cryptographic hash plus only its last four digits. We also store the transfer amount, TrxID, timestamps, linked bridge device identifier, claim status, and the authenticated account that claims the resulting Support Points.

How we use information

  • To authenticate your account and keep submitted tickets and game history associated with you.
  • To display your ticket history, game history, balances, referrals, and simulated results.
  • To verify development-support transfers and prevent duplicate claims.
  • To operate, secure, troubleshoot, and improve the application.
  • To prevent unauthorized access to another user's private records.

Public and private data

General event/game information and public event results may be visible publicly. Account-linked tickets, instant-game history, balances, referral records, Support Point balances, support claims, device tokens, and transaction verification records are protected through authenticated access controls or server-side administration.

Service providers

The application uses Google for OAuth sign-in, Supabase for authentication/database/server functions, and GitHub Pages for website hosting. bKash is not integrated as a gameplay payment gateway. The Phone Bridge is used only for the separate development-support record system.

Data retention

Account, ticket, spin, ledger, referral, support-verification, and Support Point records may remain stored while this testing project is active. Because this is an experimental project, records may also be reset or deleted during development and testing.

Security

We use authentication, row-level database access controls, server-side functions, transaction locking, hashed device tokens, hashed sender numbers, one-time transaction claiming, and server-side game result generation to reduce unauthorized access and manipulation. No internet service can guarantee absolute security.

Children

This application is intended for general software testing and demonstration. It is not designed as a real-money gambling service or a service directed to children.

Your choices

You may stop using the service at any time. If you want to request removal of account-linked test data, contact the project owner through the project's public GitHub repository.

Changes

This policy may be updated as the application changes. The latest version will remain available at this URL.
