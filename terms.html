Test tickets and results

Submitted tickets are digital test records only. Each event maintains its own ticket pool. When an event is drawn, the backend selects the configured number of winner tickets only from tickets already submitted to that event and applies the configured rank-specific virtual-credit prizes. Simulated results have no monetary value and cannot be redeemed.

Development support and Support Points

The project may accept voluntary development support outside the draw system. A linked Phone Bridge may verify a received-money bKash notification by matching the sender number and TrxID. A verified transfer can create an equal number of separate Support Points at the displayed conversion rate. Support Points are a supporter record only: they cannot purchase draw tickets, increase winning odds, create prize eligibility, be withdrawn, be transferred for value, or be converted to cash or draw credits.
