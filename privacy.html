Privacy Policy

How DRAW//01 handles account information and ticket data when you use this public simulation and testing application.

Last updated: October 4, 2026

DRAW//01 is a simulation/testing project. It does not sell lottery tickets, accept wagers, process payments, or award cash prizes.

Information we collect

If you sign in with Google, the authentication provider may provide basic account information such as your name, email address, profile image, and a unique account identifier. We also store the simulated event tickets you submit, including selected numbers, event association, timestamps, winner rank, and simulated virtual-credit results.

How we use information

  • To authenticate your account and keep your submitted tickets associated with you.
  • To display your ticket history and simulated draw results.
  • To operate, secure, troubleshoot, and improve the application.
  • To prevent unauthorized access to another user's private ticket data.

Public and private data

Event results and general event information are public. Your account-linked ticket records are protected through database access controls and are intended to be visible to your authenticated account and authorized administrators.

Service providers

The application uses Google for OAuth sign-in, Supabase for authentication/database services, and GitHub Pages for website hosting. Those providers may process technical data under their own privacy terms.

Data retention

Account and ticket data may remain stored while this testing project is active. Because this is an experimental project, records may also be reset or deleted during development and testing.

Security

We use authentication, row-level database access controls, and server-side draw logic to reduce unauthorized access. No internet service can guarantee absolute security.

Children

This application is intended for general software testing and demonstration. It is not designed as a gambling service or a service directed to children.

Your choices

You may stop using the service at any time. If you want to request removal of account-linked test data, contact the project owner through the project's public GitHub repository.

Changes

This policy may be updated as the application changes. The latest version will remain available at this URL.

Contact

For privacy questions, use the public contact information linked from the About & Contact page.
