Privacy Policy

How DRAW//01 handles account, ticket, and development-support verification data.

Last updated: October 5, 2026

DRAW//01 is a simulation/testing project. Draw credits have no cash value. Separate development-support transfers may be verified only to record non-draw Support Points, which cannot be used for tickets, odds, prizes, withdrawals, or cash conversion.

Information we collect

If you sign in with Google, the authentication provider may provide basic account information such as your name, email address, profile image, and a unique account identifier. We also store simulated event tickets you submit, including selected numbers, event association, timestamps, winner rank, and simulated virtual-credit results.

Development-support verification

If the Phone Bridge feature is used, the system processes a received-money bKash SMS to identify the transfer amount, TrxID, sender number, and received time. The full SMS text is not stored in the transaction database. The sender number is normalized and stored as a cryptographic hash plus only its last four digits. We also store the transfer amount, TrxID, timestamps, linked bridge device identifier, claim status, and the authenticated account that claims the resulting Support Points.

How we use information

  • To authenticate your account and keep your submitted tickets associated with you.
  • To display your ticket history and simulated draw results.
  • To verify development-support transfers and prevent the same TrxID from being claimed more than once.
  • To operate, secure, troubleshoot, and improve the application.
  • To prevent unauthorized access to another user's private records.

Public and private data

Event results and general event information are public. Account-linked tickets, Support Point balances, support claims, device tokens, and transaction verification records are protected through authenticated access controls or server-side administration. Device tokens are stored as hashes in the database.

Service providers

The application uses Google for OAuth sign-in, Supabase for authentication/database/server functions, and GitHub Pages for website hosting. bKash is not integrated as a payment gateway in this feature; the Phone Bridge verifies a transfer notification received on the linked phone. These providers and services may process technical data under their own terms and privacy practices.

Data retention

Account, ticket, support-verification, and Support Point records may remain stored while this testing project is active. Because this is an experimental project, records may also be reset or deleted during development and testing.

Security

We use authentication, row-level database access controls, server-side functions, hashed device tokens, hashed sender numbers, and one-time transaction claiming to reduce unauthorized access and duplicate claims. No internet service can guarantee absolute security.

Children

This application is intended for general software testing and demonstration. It is not designed as a gambling service or a service directed to children.

Your choices

You may stop using the service at any time. If you want to request removal of account-linked test data, contact the project owner through the project's public GitHub repository.

This policy may be updated as the application changes. The latest version will remain available at this URL.
